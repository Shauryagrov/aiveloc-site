About

A studio of one, shipping deliberately.

Aiveloc is an independent iOS app studio founded and run by Shaurya Grover, who started the company in 8th grade. The bet: a small team using AI can punch above its weight — without losing the human taste that makes a product feel cared-for.

Every app on the shelf is shipped by hand. Every screen, every interaction, every word — reviewed by a real human before it goes out.
